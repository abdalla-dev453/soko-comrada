# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **soko-comrada** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our application, services, or interact with our repository.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** If applicable, account registration details such as usernames, email addresses, and profile information.
* **Communications:** Any information shared when reaching out for support, reporting vulnerabilities, or submitting feedback.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Log Data:** Standard web hosting logs, including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, referring/exit pages, and timestamps.
* **Usage Details:** Telemetry data related to how features within the platform are utilized to help improve service stability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following essential purposes:
* **Service Provision:** To maintain, secure, optimize, and operate the platform.
* **Security & Vulnerability Patching:** Tracking issues or reports securely as outlined in our `SECURITY.md` guidelines.
* **Compliance & Legal Obligations:** Meeting necessary regional data protection and security criteria.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared under the following conditions:
* **Hosting Partners:** Infrastructure platforms used to deploy our services (e.g., [Render](https://render.com), GitHub).
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, regulation, or a binding legal process.

---

## 4. Security of Your Data

The security of your data is important to us. We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you may hold specific rights regarding your personal information:
* The right to **access** or update your personal data.
* The right to request the **deletion** of your data.
* The right to **object** to or restrict certain processing activities.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
